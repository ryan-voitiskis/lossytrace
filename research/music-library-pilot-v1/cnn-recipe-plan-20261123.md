# Offline synthetic recipe holdouts

Fixed before CNN training or scoring. The earlier measurement results are known. This exploratory comparison covers the identity processing condition only, with one synthetic baseline and six decoded codec copies for each of 24 compositions. Each fold trains on three complete recipes and holds out the fourth. All crops and codec variants follow their source. The split assigns synthetic indices 0–5, 6–11, 12–17 and 18–23 to recipes 0–3 respectively.

Use the existing fixed-seed compact CNN and native-rate mid/side frontend. As a laptop-scale variant, average-pool each four-second spectrogram to 128 frequency bands by 32 time positions before the CNN. Use six crops beginning at 0, 23, 46, 69, 92 and 116 seconds. Fit six epochs of AdamW (0.001, weight decay 0.0001), balanced binary cross entropy. Each epoch includes every training source/codec pair once, paired with its baseline at the same crop. Cycle crop index by (source index + codec index + zero-based epoch) modulo six. Shuffle pairs with the candidate seed per fold, maintained across epochs, six pairs per optimizer step. Score the arithmetic mean of all six window logits per file after the final epoch.

Fit the existing fixed logistic comparator once per fold on the same training sources and seven identity states using full-duration saved measurements and recipe weighting. Use logit >= 0 for both. Record per-fold sensitivity, specificity, balanced accuracy and positive detections per codec, then the equally weighted mean balanced accuracy over recipes. Four fits per model, no tuning or threshold search. This compares the two pipelines with different audio coverage (24 versus 120 seconds). File counts include related variants. Real-music performance and generalization to subsequent processing remain open.
