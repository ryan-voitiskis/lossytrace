The remaining file entry for *Primrose Polka* omits a license field. None of the 40 original file records supplies sample rate, bit depth or channel count. Duration and size cannot uniquely identify those three quantities.

Linking exact normalized performers (including comma-delimited joint credits), named accompanists, the Duncan McMillan Trio and shared tune titles inside medleys reduces the 40 items to **20 provisional components**. The 11-item largest component connects Duncan McMillan, Tony Capaldi and David Hutchison via *Atholl Highlanders*. Its edges concern shared works or performers; these recordings need not come from one session. Also holding together the differently credited tracks from compilation LPs HMV DLP 1143 and EMI CLP 1220 reduces the count to 18. That last calculation is a sensitivity check, since an LP can contain multiple sessions. Even the figure of 20 is below the 30-group minimum for an evaluation stratum. This bound applies to the shortlist; additional items could introduce new groups or link existing groups. The catalogue cannot establish that unnamed performers or sessions are distinct.

## Follow-up on the upstream Potpourri collection

The archive's advanced search for `collection:hifidelity_potpourri AND mediatype:audio AND date:[* TO 1922-12-31]` returned 876 records and 500 distinct nonempty literal creator fields. The date is the provider's index field. The query included nonmusic: an eight-item diagnostic sample, ordered by the SHA-256 of `lossytrace-potpourri-intake-v1:` plus identifier, contained seven items with original WAVs and one MP3 audiobook. [Archive collection](https://archive.org/details/hifidelity_potpourri)

The complete index contains 145 CC0 URL declarations. The metadata API returned an identity- and license-matching record for 141; four responses lacked concordant metadata and are excluded. **137 items** list both an original WAVE and a FLAC derivative explicitly naming the WAV parent with a duration of at least 30 seconds. All 137 descriptions contain a cartridge, transfer-EQ or declicking cue. These items have 91 distinct nonempty creator field values. For example, *Aloha Oe / Hawai Ponoi* lists Arthur Pryor's Band, Victor 65440-B, cartridge compensation, a 5 kHz low-pass filter and de-clicking. This type of intentionally bandwidth-limited performance matters for measuring false alerts. [Item metadata](https://archive.org/metadata/aloha-oe-hawai-ponoi)

The CC0 declaration is item-level evidence. Transfer text was screened mechanically and is not sufficient by itself to certify every item's chain, all underlying rights, technical eligibility, music completeness or freshness. A contemporary digital transfer can have different rights from its underlying composition or performance. The 91 field values still include shared singers, quartets, variant spellings and unidentified accompanists. An archive mirror is not a new production stratum. The most valuable next check is to link a stratified set of named recording matrices to independent session/performer metadata and determine how many usable components survive. No new source was allocated, scored or counted toward release acceptance.
