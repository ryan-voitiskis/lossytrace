# Permissive multi-provider source candidates - 2026-08-13

**Status:** current official metadata exposes enough raw grouped capacity to
justify exact-member qualification. No source group is yet qualified or
allocated for listening truth; no new audio access, retained-reference read,
projection, stimulus generation, metric execution, recruitment, response
collection, no-reference work, or public verdict is authorized.

## Outcome

The 16-source one-provider ODAQ path cannot reach the frozen asymptotic
equivalence-power target. The score-blind frontier requires at least 39
independent source groups in every truth-bearing partition: 156 unique groups
across development, calibration, transfer, and final validation. The practical
120-source sensitivity would require 480.

Current CC BY metadata is not the limiting factor by count alone. Seven
providers in the exact-member-audit tier expose 1,867 conservative candidate
groups:

| Slakh2100-redux | deduplicated MIDI UUID | 1,710 | synthesized music, not real mastered music |
| VCTK clean | speaker | 56 | studio speech |
| Lombard Grid | talker | 54 | laboratory speech |
| SATP | recording coordinate | 25 | binaural urban soundscape |
| SONYC-Backgrounds | sensor | 15 | urban sensor soundscape |
| GuitarSet | performer | 6 | solo guitar only |
| TinySOL | recording program | 1 | isolated notes; one coordinated corpus |

All counts are metadata-level capacity. `qualified_reference_group_count` and
`allocated_group_count` remain zero. In particular, 1,710 derivatives from one
synthesis provider cannot prove provider transfer, and a lossless FLAC or PCM
WAV container cannot prove that an upstream real recording was never
previously lossily coded.

## Pending and development-only candidates

MusicNet exposes 330 classical recordings and 134 conservative
work-performance groups after grouping the official metadata by composer,
composition, upstream source, and transcriber. Its PCM-WAV container does not
resolve upstream coding history, so it remains pending rather than entering
the exact-member-audit tier.

FSD50K contains 43,379 clips whose item-level licence metadata is CC0 or CC BY,
grouped conservatively into 6,111 uploaders. It remains a natural-negative or
pending pool: the collection is mixed-license and crowdsourced, and neither
clip-level permission nor original coding history can be inferred from the
collection-level record.

NSynth remains a 16 kHz bandwidth-limited synthetic-note development negative.
The 16 retained ODAQ clean references remain the already accepted
one-provider, development-only path. Neither is promoted to fresh transfer or
final validation.

## Required allocation discipline

No source group may cross partitions. The grouping boundary includes the same
work, performance, session, duplicate MIDI, uploader, speaker, sensor, and
derived or remixed relatives. A provider used for a provider-held-out claim
must be wholly absent outside its holdout partition; a provider split across
partitions does not count as held out.

The later full-reference evaluation still requires at least eight source
groups in every prespecified primary domain. There is currently no qualified
real mastered, multi-genre music provider pool, so the raw total cannot repair
that missing domain or provider independence.

## Decision

The candidate inventory clears only a metadata-capacity screen. It does not
establish four-partition feasibility. The next score-blind step is a bounded
exact-member metadata and allocation proposal covering immutable versions,
rights and attribution, original coding history, deduplication, domain
balance, whole-provider holdouts, duration, storage, rendering, and balanced
incomplete-block workload.

That proposal must remain metadata-only unless a responsible human separately
authorizes new audio acquisition. The user's existing ODAQ authorization is
not broadened by this checkpoint.
