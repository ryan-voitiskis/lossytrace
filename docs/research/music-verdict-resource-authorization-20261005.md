# Continued source research without fixed resource caps

On 5 October 2026 the user authorized removing the budget and continuing the
LossyTrace work with the resources needed. The prior metadata-search ceiling
no longer prevents source qualification. The fixed download, working-storage
and local machine-time caps are also removed; headroom, load and no-clobber
checks remain required. This is not authority for paid services, outreach,
account creation, publication or merging.

The user subsequently approved temporary laptop storage while away from the
schwarz drive for several weeks. Qualified research audio and generated data
may use the Git-ignored `.tmp/music-verdict-v1` folder, preserving at least
100 GiB of filesystem free space. Retained data should move to schwarz when
available. This local-storage exception does not change source or execution
gates and does not authorize removal of unrelated files. The initial check
showed approximately 183 GiB free; future work must check again rather than
treat that observation as reserved capacity.

The [machine-readable amendment](../../research/music-verdict-v1/resource-authorization-20261005.json)
supersedes only those resource caps in the
[original design](music-verdict-successor-20260927.md). The original design
bytes and all prior observations remain preserved. No successor audio has
been acquired or scored, and neither model has been trained at this amendment.

The supported population, clean-history evidence, rights checks, independent
source counts, source separation, sealed-data exclusions and numerical release
gates are unchanged. The single candidate, comparator, seed, 20-epoch ceiling
and prohibition on scientific retries are experimental controls, not resource
caps being removed. A source shortage remains a feasibility finding, not a
negative model result.

Next work is public, metadata-only source intake: examine individual recording
histories, source terms and performer/session relationships, starting with the
Live Music Archive and other original-recording collections. Availability or
a FLAC suffix does not qualify an item. Audio acquisition remains conditional
on source qualification; model predictions remain conditional on the separate
execution freeze.
