# Mastered-music and playback qualification - 2026-08-04

**Status:** source candidates and current local output observed; licence,
independent-provider, excerpt-manifest, and physical-playback gates remain open

## Source result

The strongest mastered reference remains the 2026 [RWC Music Database
release](https://zenodo.org/records/18656623). Its companion [dataset
paper](https://transactions.ismir.net/articles/10.5334/tismir.326) says the
13.4 GB release contains original master-quality lossless recordings across
popular, genre, jazz, classical, and royalty-free subcollections. Existing
LossyTrace evidence already binds all 328 WAV payloads and 85 eligible artist
families, but RWC is still one provider stratum and its CC BY-NC 4.0 terms need
a responsible-human noncommercial-use decision before listening delivery.

[MAESTRO 3.0.0](https://magenta.withgoogle.com/datasets/maestro) is a separate
high-fidelity music source: 1,276 uncompressed 44.1–48 kHz stereo piano
performances under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. It can add separately grouped acoustic
performance calibration after every previously consumed composition is
excluded, but it is not evidence of broad mastered production. [mshoxxDB
1.2](https://zenodo.org/records/19599752) can add a small electronic-production
stratum with documented full-mix processing, but its 18 mixtures are mono,
come from one artist, and use the same NC-SA licence family.

[MusicNet](https://zenodo.org/records/5120004) is deferred because its record
describes 330 PCM WAV recordings from three origins but does not state a
dataset-level licence; exact item provenance, licensing, and prior-lossy lineage
must be audited. [MedleyDB](https://medleydb.weebly.com/downloads.html) is
deferred because access is requested and the provider asks for consent before
republishing any part. The [FMA dataset](https://github.com/mdeff/fma) is
explicitly MP3-encoded and per-track licensed, and [MTG-Jamendo's Zenodo
release](https://zenodo.org/records/3826813) contains metadata rather than a
lossless reference corpus. Those two may be natural hard negatives, not clean
codec-generation references.

This evidence supports only a future narrow claim around professional RWC
productions with separate piano/electronic calibration strata. It does not
support a broad commercial-music or all-genre claim.

There is now a separately bound
[`CC BY/CC0-only fallback`](perceptual-degradation-permissive-listening-source-fallback-20260804.md).
It retains 16 previously audited clean ODAQ references: nine music excerpts and
seven movie-like soundtrack excerpts, conservatively grouped into 13 upstream
works. All attribution metadata is complete and no NC or SA source is present.
This avoids a noncommercial determination for development listening, but ODAQ
remains one provider stratum. Its published conditions simulate coding-like
artifacts or source-separation/remix damage; they are not actual codec encodes,
grouped transfer evidence, or final validation.

## Playback result

The current default output is the built-in MacBook Pro speakers, stereo at
44.1 kHz. No external DAC/amplifier, headphone model, ambient-noise class,
system-effects state, or conservative level-calibration method is observable.
The earlier confirmation that all generated synthetic sounds were audible
remains a player-plumbing check; it is not physical playback qualification or
listening truth.

The playback gate therefore remains open. A responsible operator must declare
a stable headphone or calibrated loudspeaker chain and complete the frozen
channel/calibration checks before any real listener session. No degradation
ratings or participant data are needed for that check.

## Next gate

Two human decisions remain: choose the narrower permissive one-provider path
or the wider NC/SA source track, and declare/qualify the physical playback
chain. The permissive path avoids an NC/SA determination but narrows the
supportable source-breadth claim. Until a path is chosen, audio access, excerpt
listening, stimulus generation, metrics, participant contact, and human
collection remain unauthorized.
