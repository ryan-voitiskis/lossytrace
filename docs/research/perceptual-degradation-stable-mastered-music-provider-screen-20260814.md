# Stable mastered-music provider screen - 2026-08-14

**Status:** a bounded official-record search found three immutable CC BY 4.0
artist-release candidates with enough conservatively grouped WAV capacity to
join AlbumDB across four provider-pure partitions. Stable-only mastered-music
arithmetic is now feasible and no longer depends on mutable Bandcamp pages.
This is not source selection or scientific eligibility. No archive, archive
member, audio member, waveform, metric, score, listener response or sealed
evidence was opened.

## Retained candidates

### Solar Flux

[Album One](https://zenodo.org/records/21454470) and
[Album Two](https://zenodo.org/records/21454972) are separate CC BY 4.0 records
from creator Adrian Ashmore. Each exposes eight individually checksummed WAV
objects, for a 16-track public-record ceiling across the one creator provider.
All duplicate, alternate, derivative or shared-composition relationships must
collapse during exact audit.

The records do not document performers, instruments, synthesis or generation
method, recording chain, mixing, mastering, or the creator-depositor
relationship beyond the structured creator field. They are therefore stable
artist-labelled album candidates, not established mastered or never-lossy
truth.

### Lotte Lehmann farewell recital

The [Santa Barbara farewell-recital record](https://zenodo.org/records/14226540)
is CC BY 4.0 and names Lotte Lehmann and Gwendolyn Koldofsky as rights holders,
alongside institutional collectors and curators. It documents a 1951 recital
first publicly released as a limited historical album in 1977, later recovered
from a USC collection and digitized by a University of North Texas sound
preservationist.

The public description says 21 WAV files, while the current immutable record
exposes 19 individually checksummed WAV objects totalling 565,304,036 bytes.
The conservative capacity is 19 named composition performances within one
recital, performer pair and historical release chain. The 21-versus-19
discrepancy must be reconciled before exact-member eligibility.

### Remnant Tamil Worship

[Album 1 HD](https://zenodo.org/records/20969062) and
[Album 2 HD](https://zenodo.org/records/21020982) are CC BY 4.0 multilingual
worship releases from Tamil Intercessor Remnant / KJC-DNN. Their public
descriptions identify six compositions per album and expose checksummed WAV
distributions.

The 55 plus 60 WAV objects do not create 115 groups. Every language,
translation, vocal version, instrumental, render or derivative of one
composition collapses, leaving a 12-composition public-record ceiling across
both albums. The records do not document performers, synthesis or generation
methods, recording chain, mixing, mastering, or whether each WAV descends from
an independent lossless origin.

## Arithmetic consequence

The three providers add 47 metadata-level mastered-release groups. The stable
pool now has 20 providers and 2,285 candidate groups at DataSTORRE's recording
ceiling, or 2,226 at its conservative floor. The broader public-record pool has
24 providers and 2,375 groups at the ceiling, or 2,316 at the floor.

The provider-pure solver can now assign AlbumDB, Solar Flux, the Lehmann
historical album, and Remnant Tamil Worship to separate partitions with at
least eight mastered-music groups in each. The existing stable-only
three-domain and four-120-group sensitivities remain feasible. ICSI and the
three Bandcamp releases are no longer required for any currently modelled
arithmetic repair.

Qualified and allocated counts remain zero. A capacity witness does not choose
members, prove independence, validate rights or origin chains, or establish
that the candidate WAV names identify uncompressed PCM.

## Screened but not added

  eight-group capacity is unproven.

- Solar Flux Album Three is MP3-only and contributes no clean lossless
  capacity.
- Tin Men and the Telephone's located Zenodo catalog exposes only two separate
  lossless WAV works; the other album objects are MP3 or video.

## Boundary and next decision

No source or metric successor is selected. The screen does not authorize
preservation, exact-member inspection, archive-member listing, audio
acquisition or access, retained ODAQ access or projection, stimulus generation,
metric execution, scores, listener collection, sealed evidence, no-reference
training, or a public verdict.

The remaining source-side decision is whether to authorize bounded preservation
and exact-member metadata audit of the stable records, or reject the current
truth-source design. Production and mastering-chain gaps must remain explicit
during that audit; stable arithmetic alone cannot promote these records to
clean truth.
