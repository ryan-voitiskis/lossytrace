# Perceptual-degradation sparse non-tonal metadata search v2 - 2026-08-19

**Status:** frozen before successor discovery queries or new exact-record
inspection.

This checkpoint permits one bounded metadata-only search for an exact sparse
non-tonal successor. It binds the consumed Freesound abstention, the five later
metadata rejections, the frozen descriptor, and the current objective audit.

The search is capped at eight declared discovery queries and twenty primary
exact-record inspections. Search snippets can locate provider records but are
not evidence. Only text or JSON from Freesound or Zenodo primary records may
support eligibility, and the search stops at the first fully eligible exact
member.

Eligibility remains unchanged: CC0 1.0 or CC BY 4.0, an explicitly
provider-original lossless WAV of at least 2.000 seconds, one natural
transient, an explicit capture chain for every channel, and an explicit
no-processing or trim-only history. Silence, `raw`, or an unspecified history
is insufficient. The six consumed exact members are excluded.

Candidate pages are fetched as text without loading referenced media. Audio,
preview players, waveform or spectrogram assets, downloads, descriptors,
playback, metrics, source-trait assignment, manifest allocation, listening,
training, sealed evidence and public verdicts remain closed.

Finding a metadata-qualified member nominates it only. Audio access requires a
new immutable exact-member checkpoint, a successful exact-head CI run, and the
separate one-worker confirmation protocol. A bounded negative result is valid.
