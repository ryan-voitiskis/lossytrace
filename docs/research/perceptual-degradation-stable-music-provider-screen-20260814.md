# Stable music-provider screen - 2026-08-14

**Status:** the bounded official-record search found two stable CC BY 4.0
controlled-music candidates, but no stable mastered-release replacement for
the three preservation-required Bandcamp records. Vienna 4x22 and ROD improve
controlled real-performance breadth; neither is promoted to mastered-music
evidence. No archive, audio member, member listing, metric, score, listener
response or sealed evidence was opened.

## Retained candidates

The [Vienna 4x22 corpus](https://datasets.mdw.ac.at/datasets/dataset/98ea25fa-2468-43ff-929b-3c926e163583)
is a versioned CC BY 4.0 record containing 22 professional pianists playing
four classical excerpts. The official record documents a named studio,
Bösendorfer Imperial piano, stereo AKG CK91 ORTF microphones, Tascam DA-P1
digital audio tape recorder, and a fixed 1,295,130,820-byte
[audio distribution](https://datasets.mdw.ac.at/datasets/distribution/106a4eba-4ebe-424f-a284-0ede77de7d68?view=full).
The conservative pre-audit grouping is 22 performers across all works. The
record does not expose a checksum or establish the delivered member encoding,
and one studio/piano/recording chain remains a narrow controlled-performance
stratum—not a mastered release.

The immutable [Raga Ornamentation Detection record](https://zenodo.org/records/17851882)
is CC BY 4.0 and binds one 2,423,991,916-byte archive with MD5
`6fb3ff4e19bb38742f770b407258476b`. It describes 212 44.1 kHz, 16-bit mono
PCM-WAV recordings from two expert Indian classical vocalists, including
studio-quality teacher material and broadcast renditions. The conservative
capacity is two performer groups. Studio and broadcast origin chains,
accompaniment and session relationships remain unaudited, and this is not a
mastered multigenre release.

Together they add two stable providers and 24 metadata-level controlled-music
groups. Qualified and allocated counts remain zero.

## Why the mastered-music repair failed

The strongest lossless multitrack or mixed candidates did not pass the narrow
rights and role gates:

- [The Spheres Dataset](https://zenodo.org/records/17347681) and
  [FreiDi](https://zenodo.org/records/20285754) are CC BY-SA 4.0.
- [KRAISLER](https://zenodo.org/records/21082251) has a structured CC BY 4.0
  field but its public description separately prohibits commercial use and
  performer defamation. Plain CC BY cannot be inferred while those additional
  restrictions remain.
- MoisesDB's paper is CC BY, but the
  [audio repository](https://github.com/moises-ai/moises-db) is CC BY-NC-SA
  4.0. A paper licence does not license the audio.
- Hi-Audio's Happy Birthday material is a mutable platform collection around
  one work, not a stable mastered multigenre provider.
- MUSAN does not expose a clean lossless origin chain for its compiled music at
  collection level. FMA distributes MP3 and cannot supply clean lossless
  references.

This is a bounded negative search result, not a claim that no suitable public
record exists anywhere. It does show that the new stable candidates cannot
replace the mutable mastered-release dependency found in the preceding
screen.

## Corrected arithmetic consequence

Adding the 24 controlled-music groups changes the count-only sensitivity. The
full public-record pool now reaches 20 providers and 2,140 groups at the
DataSTORRE recording ceiling, or 2,081 at its conservative eight-group floor.
Both can arithmetically fill four 120-group partitions. That repair still
depends on preservation-required ICSI and Bandcamp records, unaudited source
relationships, and no exact member selection. The stable-only pool has 16
providers and 2,050 groups at the ceiling, but only 340 non-Slakh groups, so it
still cannot fill the three other 120-group partitions. Stable-only
three-domain and mastered-music sensitivities also remain infeasible.

## Boundary and next decision

No source or metric successor is selected. The screen does not authorize
preservation, exact-member inspection, audio acquisition, audio-member access,
retained ODAQ access or projection, stimulus generation, metric execution,
scores, listener collection, sealed evidence, no-reference training, or a
public verdict. The responsible-human source decision remains: authorize a
bounded preservation and exact-member metadata audit, narrow the mastered-
music or primary-domain claim, or reject the current truth-source design.
