# Listener information, consent, and privacy draft - 2026-08-03

**Status:** preparation draft only; do not recruit, consent, or collect data

## What this study is

You would listen to short audio excerpts and compare an original with altered
versions. The study investigates whether changes are audible and how much they
affect perceived quality. It does not test your musical taste, diagnose your
hearing, or ask you to identify an audio codec.

Participation is voluntary. A session is expected to take 20-30 minutes,
including training and breaks. Some sounds may contain obvious distortion,
noise, bandwidth limitation, pre-echo, clipping, or other production changes.

## Possible discomfort and safety

Listening at a comfortable level presents low foreseeable risk, but prolonged
or critical listening can cause fatigue, annoyance, headache, or temporary
awareness of tinnitus. Set the calibration at a comfortable level and do not
increase it during the test. You may lower the level, take a break, skip the
remaining session, or withdraw at any time. Stop immediately if you experience
pain, marked discomfort, dizziness, or worsening tinnitus. The study is not
medical care; seek appropriate professional care for a health concern.

## Data collected

The response system uses a random participant code. It records:

- comparison choices and quality ratings;
- anonymous stimulus, assignment, and session codes;
- replay and switching counts and coarse response timing;
- whether playback, channel, level, and training checks passed; and
- coarse device, software, transducer, and room/noise categories needed to
  interpret listening conditions.

It must not record your name, email, account name, raw IP address, precise
location, filenames, local paths, device serial numbers, audio from your
microphone, or unrelated activity. If contact or payment details are needed,
they are stored separately by a human coordinator and cannot be joined to
responses by the analysis dataset alone.

## Use, access, and publication

Pseudonymous individual responses are available only to the approved research
team for quality control and source/listener-aware statistical analysis.
Public repository artifacts contain code, protocols, empty schemas, and
path-free aggregates only. No raw individual response, contact detail, private
audio, or small-cell breakdown is published. Aggregate release requires the
privacy threshold frozen before recruitment.

The final authorization must state the encrypted storage location, people with
access, retention duration, backup/deletion behavior, breach contact, and any
overseas data handling. Those fields are deliberately unresolved in this
draft; recruitment cannot begin while any is blank.

## Withdrawal and deletion

You may stop without giving a reason. Before the de-identification cutoff, you
may request deletion using the withdrawal code shown after consent. The contact
channel and cutoff date must be filled in before recruitment. After responses
have been irreversibly aggregated and the link to the withdrawal code deleted,
removing one person's contribution may no longer be possible; the final form
must state that date clearly.

Stopping or withdrawing does not affect any relationship with the researchers.
The final compensation terms must say whether partial participation is paid;
they cannot be changed based on a participant's answers or test performance.

## Consent statements

Before a session, the participant must actively confirm each statement:

1. I am eligible under the age and jurisdiction rules stated on the final form.
2. I have read the study purpose, duration, tasks, risks, and data handling.
3. I understand that this is not a medical hearing test.
4. I know I can stop, take a break, or withdraw without giving a reason.
5. I know what data are collected and that contact/payment data are separate.
6. I know the withdrawal method, deadline, and limits after aggregation.
7. I consent to the described research use of my pseudonymous responses.

Consent is versioned and timestamped separately from trial data. No box is
preselected. Declining ends the flow without creating a response record.

## Fields required before authorization

- responsible human researcher and contact;
- complaints or independent contact, if applicable;
- governing organization and applicable ethics/privacy basis;
- eligibility jurisdiction and minimum age;
- storage system, region, encryption, access list, and incident process;
- raw and aggregate retention periods and deletion method;
- withdrawal contact, code procedure, and de-identification cutoff;
- compensation amount, currency, partial-session rule, and payment operator;
- minimum public aggregation cell size and suppression policy; and
- protocol, software, consent, privacy, and analysis version hashes.

No real participant may be approached until every field is resolved and the
human-collection gate is explicitly changed in a committed score-blind record.
