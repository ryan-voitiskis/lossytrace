# Perceptual-degradation sparse/tonal exact-member confirmation - 2026-08-19

**Status:** the two frozen exact members were measured twice under the
predeclared, score-free descriptor. The private reports are byte-identical, no
decoded or derived PCM was retained, and attribution is attached privately.

The TinySOL oboe passed the tonal non-sparse descriptor on its supported
channel: all ten time blocks were active, the median spectral flatness was
`0.000027177`, and the median top-eight-bin power share was `0.862668303`.
This is a technical descriptor confirmation, not a source-trait assignment or
perceptual claim.

The Freesound finger snap did not pass the sparse non-tonal descriptor. Both
channels produced the same result: three of ten time blocks were active, while
the frozen sparse rule permits at most two, and the spectrum met the tonal
rather than non-tonal rule. The correct terminal outcome is abstention. The
candidate was not replaced and no threshold was changed after observation.

An independent audit confirmed that the public result is the exact projection
of two byte-identical private replays, contains no private path or audio hash,
and preserves the attribution attachment. The negative result remains useful:
it rules out this exact member under the frozen descriptor without turning a
near miss into a positive.

This checkpoint does not establish an independent sparse/tonal pair, assign a
source trait, freeze or allocate a source manifest, generate a codec, execute a
perceptual metric, perform playback, collect a response, open sealed evidence,
