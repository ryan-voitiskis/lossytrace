# Perceptual-degradation source and condition qualification - 2026-08-04

**Status:** metadata-only candidates qualified; source/condition manifest,
licences, audio access, stimulus generation, metric execution, recruitment,
and human collection remain unfrozen or unauthorized

## Outcome

The existing public source audits can support score-blind planning across
speech, isolated instruments, natural soundscapes, bandwidth-limited material,
and grouped source/domain/encoder transfer. They do not yet support freezing a
listening manifest. In particular, the available CC BY source set does not
provide multiple independently grouped mastered-music collections, and the
strongest existing mastered-music candidate is CC BY-NC 4.0 and still needs a
study-distribution review.

The machine-readable
[`source-condition qualification plan`](../../benchmarks/perceptual-degradation-v1/source-condition-qualification-plan.json)
binds only committed, path-free repository evidence. It did not open retained
audio, provider audio, waveforms, listening results, perceptual scores, private
source identities, the 280-case future subset, or release holdout labels.

## Source boundary

Five previously audited collections are candidates under CC BY 4.0:

- VCTK clean speech: 56 conservative speaker groups;
- TinySOL isolated instruments: one conservative collection group;
- SONYC-Backgrounds: 15 sensor groups;
- SATP soundscapes: 25 coordinate groups; and
- Lombard Grid: 54 talker groups.

FSDD is retained only as a bandwidth-limited hard-negative candidate pending a
CC BY-SA distribution review. RAVDESS and RWC remain candidates pending their
noncommercial/share-alike operational reviews. RWC contributes the needed
mastered-music breadth, but its one provider stratum cannot by itself establish
independent music-collection transfer.

The 527 already-consumed v1 groups are not promoted to independent listening
truth. The previously sealed future and release-held-out history labels remain
sealed and are not perceptual labels.

ODAQ remains a 16-group, development-only source of published simulated
artifacts. Its selected attribution metadata is complete, but its published
conditions are not actual codec encodes and cannot serve as grouped transfer
or fresh final validation.

## Condition boundary

The plan binds 12 development and eight encoder-transfer templates across MP3,
AAC-LC, Opus, and Vorbis. These are recipe candidates, not quality labels.
Codec, encoder, nominal bitrate, and nominal quality are forbidden targets.

The version-2 listening schema uses `transparency_candidate`, not
`transparent_codec`. The historical version-1 schema and allocator remain
byte-identical for their already-recorded player observations. A high-quality
setting is only a hypothesis submitted to the frozen listening analysis. It
becomes a transparent, non-degraded control only if that evidence supports the
preregistered equivalence conclusion; otherwise the result is audible or
indeterminate. This avoids building the desired answer into the stimulus
manifest without rewriting historical evidence.

Existing deterministic PCM recipes provide gain, low-pass, sample-rate
roundtrip, dither/requantization, channel-topology, and lossless-wrapper
controls. They do not yet cover the full requested control population. The
remaining score-blind recipe work is clipping, equalization, limiting,
stereo-width processing without topology change, repeated same-codec paths,
and cross-codec generations.

## Decision

Actual-codec, transparency-candidate, natural-hard-negative, and grouped
transfer candidates are now identified without assigning perceptual truth.
The source/condition manifest is not freezable because mastered-music breadth,
licence/attribution operations, missing production and multi-generation
recipes, private source-member selection, and fresh final-validation evidence
remain unresolved.

The next authorized work is score-blind recipe construction and source
licence/attribution completion. No audio or score access follows from this
qualification.
