## What changes

We can investigate an additional controlled digital coding operation using an
exactly retained input. We do not need to create another natural reference
first. The proposed baseline is one already-verified canonical integer-PCM
delivery of the 16 acquired references, all stereo at 48 kHz. The delivery
conversion and attribution remain part of their provenance; this is not a
claim of unprocessed input or total historical damage.

Those files have not been reopened or reverified for this amendment. Their
exact private inventory and current integrity must be checked in the approved
execution phase. All 16 are consumed development material from one provider;
neither 16 files nor repeated encodes establish independent transfer groups.
Keep the cohort and all derivatives together, outside final-validation evidence.

The first comparison is entirely digital:

1. Use the exact retained reference and declare any codec-input conversion.
2. Apply each fixed encoder setting and decode with bound tools.
3. Compare against the exact codec input, with a matched PCM-only control for
   any preceding conversion, preserving channel relationships and recording
   delay, padding, failures and unsupported cases.

The already-qualified MP3, AAC, Opus and Vorbis candidates are the starting
inventory, not newly selected recipes. Some historical templates use 44.1 kHz;
they cannot silently stand for native 48 kHz conditions. The execution proposal
must select a bounded matrix, multiple operating points and exact arguments
before any new waveform observation. Bitrate and encoder settings do not label
severity or transparency.

## What does not change

encoder-held-out requirements remain necessary for the broad objective. The
sparse capture moves to the deferred challenge-source work. Quiet still needs
a prospective definition; clipped still needs separate assignment and grouping.
None is promoted to a passed gate. Existing sparse outcomes and the retained
drift technical negative remain immutable.

Unsupported cases must stay visible. The shortest retained reference has
360,701 frames at 48 kHz, less than eight seconds even before assessing activity.
The existing four/eight-active-second requirements remain unchanged. Do not
pad, loop, concatenate, replace or choose excerpts after seeing results to make
a case pass. A digital timebase does not remove encoder delay, and technical
alignment is not perceptual fidelity.

The initial technical report cannot supply severity, audibility, artifact truth,
transparency or confidence intervals for those claims. Keep missing evidence
unavailable. Later human work must distinguish correct-response probability,
condition-level audibility and material impairment, using matching units and
uncertainty. A small pilot is development evidence, not powered certification.

The stopped metric gate is not reopened; the ViSQOL-only option is not selected.
ODAQ processed conditions/scores and sealed history-detection evidence remain
unopened. No-reference training still requires the full-reference pass. The
public CLI remains verdict-free. The overall research objective is incomplete.
